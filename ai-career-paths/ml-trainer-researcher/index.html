Step 6Capstone

Train-and-tune capstone

Pretrain a small language model from scratch in the CS336 style, then SFT and RL-tune it on one narrow task. Report scaling curves and eval deltas against the base model.

Follow along

  • nanochatKarpathy, free and open source. runs/speedrun.sh trains a tokenizer, pretrains a small model, fine-tunes it into a chat model and evaluates each stage; scripts/chat_rl.py adds RL on GSM8K. About 2 hours on one 8×H100 node (around $50); runs/runcpu.sh runs a tiny version on a laptop for free.
  • Reasoning from ScratchSebastian Raschka, code free (the book is paid). Chapters 6–7 train a small model with GRPO reinforcement learning on consumer hardware.
