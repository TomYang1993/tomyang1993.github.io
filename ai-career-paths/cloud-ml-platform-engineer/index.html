Cloud / ML platform engineer

Makes models fast, cheap and reliable in production: GPU clusters on any cloud, Kubernetes, inference serving, pipelines and cost control. Every other role's work ends up running on this layer.

Defining stack: Multi-cloud · PyTorch / TensorFlow · Kubernetes · Inference / serving

Step 6Capstone

Serving platform capstone

Serve an open-weights model with vLLM on Kubernetes with a GPU node pool, autoscale on queue depth, and publish a latency, throughput and cost-per-million-tokens benchmark.

Follow along

  • vLLM Production Stack tutorialsFree and open source. Follow them in order: 00 (set up Kubernetes), 01 (Helm install), 02 (basic config), 07 (benchmark on one GPU), then 20 (autoscale with KEDA on queue length, down to zero). The autoscaling step needs a cluster with at least 2 GPUs.
