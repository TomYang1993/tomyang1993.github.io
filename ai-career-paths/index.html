AI Career Path Map

Four 2026 AI role archetypes and the skills each one needs. Every skill box carries three layers: university courses, free hands-on projects, and the papers and guides behind it.

Each path is also a plain page: AI engineer · Cloud / ML platform engineer · DevOps / full-stack engineer · ML trainer / researcher. The minimal steps for each role, as plain text and links with a changelog, live in AI-career-path-minimal-steps. Role archetypes and their defining stacks start from the job-posting clusters in the AI Engineering Field Guide. Its agent-builder and RAG-builder clusters are merged here into one AI engineer role, because most postings that ask for one also ask for the other. Courses and projects were checked against their live pages in November 2026; whether lecture videos are public changes from term to term, so each course is tagged with what is open right now. Every project listed is free (some cost GPU time to run). Many courses and projects span several boxes: each card lists the others it covers. Tools are the common choices in postings, not endorsements; each is tagged open source / free (open source wins when a paid cloud also exists) or enterprise / specialized. 🔥 marks the one or two most popular courses and projects in each box: GitHub stars as of November 2026 for projects, lecture views and enrollment for courses.
