AI Front Desk

Parents ask Maple, a friendly bear clerk, about closures, menus and fees. Maple answers from each center’s own handbook, shows its sources, and hands off to staff when it should.

Built as a brightwheel take-home. Directors get a control center: see what families are asking, answer a handoff once, keep the source of truth current. Two providers back the model calls, so the demo keeps answering when a daily quota runs out. Centers, families and staff are fictional.
