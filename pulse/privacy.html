Pulse privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Pulse is a personal tool that I use only with my own YouTube channels. It is not offered to anyone else.

Data it accesses

Through Google sign-in, Pulse accesses channel details, video details, and channel and video statistics, including revenue where available. It also uploads and edits videos when I tell it to.

Where data is kept

Google sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on my computer. Statistics and video details are stored in a database on the same computer. Pulse has no server of its own.

Sharing

Pulse sends data to Google only, as needed to use its APIs. It does not sell data or send it to advertisers. If I connect an AI assistant to Pulse on my computer, that assistant can read the stored statistics and video details to answer my questions, and its provider handles them under its own terms.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions. Deleting Pulse's Keychain entries and database removes the local copy.

Third-party terms

Pulse uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
